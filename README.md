Neon Coast Cover Studio — an offline web app that turns any photo and caption into bold multi-panel GTA-poster-style cover art: six layouts (Hero + 3, Four Square, Nine Grid, Tilted Snaps, Full Bleed, Three Columns), five Vice City sunset/neon colour grades, per-panel crop and positioning by drag, scroll or sliders, several photos across the panels or one photo everywhere, editable Anton title with a Pacifico subtitle, sun, palms, halftone and grain — and one-click PNG export in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080. Everything runs from static files in your browser; photos never leave your machine. All art and lettering is original (fonts Anton & Pacifico under the SIL Open Font License) — no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — pick a photo, a layout and a grade, drag any panel to frame its photo, and hit Export PNG.
